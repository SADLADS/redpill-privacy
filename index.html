Redpill for Android

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Redpill doesn't collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

Who we are

Redpill is a medication reminder app made by SADLADS. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Redpill. If you have a question about privacy, contact us at sadlads@proton.me.

What the app stores, and where

To remind you about your medication, Redpill stores the following on your phone:

  • the medications you add: name, strength, form, the icon you choose, stock and refill level
  • their schedules and reminder times
  • your dose history: what you took, skipped or missed, and when
  • your settings, such as alarm sound, vibration and theme

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Redpill doesn't ask for the internet permission, so it can't connect to the internet at all.

Redpill turns off Android's cloud backup for its data, so it isn't copied to Google's backup servers. If you move to a new phone using Android's phone-to-phone transfer, your phone may copy the app's data directly to the new device; that happens between your own devices and doesn't involve us.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't receive your medication information, your dose history, your contact details, your location, any device identifiers or any usage statistics. Redpill contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.

Google Play may collect information about your download and use of Play itself, as described in Google's own privacy policy. That's outside the app and we don't receive anything personal from it.

Permissions the app uses

Each permission is used only on your phone, for reminders:

NotificationsShows a reminder when a dose is due, and when you're running low on a medication. POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Alarms and remindersLets reminders go off at the exact time you set, even when the phone is idle. USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Full-screen alarmsShows the medication alarm over the lock screen, like a clock app's alarm. USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT
Display over other appsOptional. Lets the alarm appear over whatever you're using and stay until you answer it. SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW
Run after restartSets your reminders again after the phone restarts. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Alarm sound and vibrationKeeps the alarm sound and vibration playing until you respond. FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, VIBRATE

Deleting your data

Because your data only exists on your phone, you're in control of it:

  • delete a medication in the app, which also deletes its history
  • clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or
  • uninstall Redpill, which deletes all of its data.

We can't recover or delete your data for you, because we never have a copy of it.

Children

Redpill is intended for adults and isn't directed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Redpill is a reminder tool. It doesn't give medical advice and doesn't replace advice from your doctor, pharmacist or other healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If the way Redpill handles information changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the app first.
